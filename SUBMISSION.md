## Name
Local Website Audit

## Short description (≤140 chars)
Plain-English one-page audit for any local business website: Google listing match, mobile, speed, local SEO, reviews, top 3 fixes.

## Long description
Give it a local business's website (and their Google listing details if you have them) and get back a friendly, one-page report a busy owner will actually read, as Markdown and PDF.

It checks:
- Does the website match the Google Business listing (hours, phone, address, website link)?
- Does it work on a phone, with a tap-to-call button?
- Speed, SSL and broken links
- Basic local SEO: page title, meta description, LocalBusiness schema, consistent name/address/phone
- A public reviews snapshot next to nearby competitors
- A clear next step: Call, Book, Order or Get a quote

No jargon. Every finding says why it costs the business customers, and the report ends with the top 3 fixes, marking which ones the owner can do free in 10 minutes. It uses public data only, never logs into anyone's Google account, and never fakes or buys reviews.

Built from the audit process Halcyon Days uses with small businesses in Fort Myers, FL. Great for web designers, marketers, consultants and owners checking their own site.
